TABLE - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026. TABLE is a multi-game app made by DevPixCode Lab ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Google or as a guest, we receive a unique account ID. With Google sign-in we also receive your name and email address.
  • Profile: the username you choose, your avatar (a letter, an emoji or your Google photo), and your language.
  • Game data: your coins, wins, losses, rank, streak, items you own, friends list, challenges and match history.
  • Messages: chat messages you send to friends and in matches.
  • Activity: a "last seen" time, used to show whether a friend is online.
  • Notifications: a push notification token for your device, plus your time zone and language, so we can send game alerts.
  • Feedback: ratings and comments you send through the in-app rating form.
  • Device and crash data: basic technical information and crash reports, used to find and fix bugs.

How we use it

To run the games, match you with friends and opponents, keep your progress and coins, show leaderboards and friend activity, send notifications you enabled, prevent cheating and abuse, and improve the app.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Other players can see your username, avatar, rank, online status and public game results. Our data is stored and processed by Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging). If ads are shown, the ad provider may use an advertising ID under its own policy and your consent choices.

Virtual coins

Coins in TABLE are virtual, are earned by playing, cannot be bought with money and have no cash value. They cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for money.

Children

TABLE is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can delete your account and its data inside the app (Profile, Delete account). You can also email devpixcode@gmail.com from your account email or with your username and we will delete your account and its data. We keep nothing beyond what the law requires. You can turn off notifications in the app or your device settings.

Security and retention

We use industry-standard protections, and access to your data is limited by security rules. We keep your data while your account exists and delete it after you delete your account.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above when we do.
